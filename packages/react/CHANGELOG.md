# @windmorph/react

All notable changes to `@windmorph/react`, newest first. Each version is grouped by kind (Added, Changed, Deprecated, Removed, Fixed, Security), and each entry links to its pull request. The [What's new](https://windmorph.dev/whats-new) page tells the same story in plain words.

## 0.2.12 — 2026-11-23

### Fixed

- A full-screen dialog (`fullScreen="always"`, or `"compact"` below 600px) paints `--md-comp-dialog-container-color`, as the basic dialog does, instead of always `surface-container-high`, while it opens too. ([#118](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/118))

## 0.2.11 — 2026-11-22

### Added

- `ListItemVideo`'s `duration` takes seconds, shown as a clock in the page's digits ("4:12", "1:02:03") and read in words ("Length 4 minutes, 12 seconds", `labels.duration` or the `LabelsProvider`'s `ListItemVideo` key); a string is still shown and read as written. `formatClock` and `formatDurationWords` in `@windmorph/react/primitives` say a media's length the same way anywhere. ([#105](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/105))
- `useReorder` in `@windmorph/react/primitives`, the engine of a list whose rows a person puts in order: a drag from a row's handle that the row follows while the rows it passes slide aside, moves by action (up, down, to the top or the bottom), cancelling, rows that can't move, and scrolling near an edge. The handle's props carry `touch-action: none`, so a finger on it drags instead of scrolling the page. It has no look and no words of its own; the core `List`'s drag-to-reorder will be built on it. ([#105](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/105))
- A `MenuGroup` can be a group of choices beside the menu's plain actions: it takes `selectionMode`, `selectedKeys`, `defaultSelectedKeys`, `onSelectionChange` and `deselectable` for its own items, which become radio or checkbox items with a selection of their own, while the menu's other items stay actions that close it. Closing follows the group's mode, as a whole selectable menu's does, and a group with `selectionMode="none"` holds plain actions, in a selectable menu too. ([#99](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/99))
- A `Menu` can show its submenus in place and open inside an element, as a video player's settings menu does. `submenuPresentation="replace"` shows a popover's submenu in place of the menu, on the same surface below a back button and the item's label; the surface keeps its corner and its side of the trigger, and its height springs to each page. `submenuTransition` fades the pages (the default) or slides them in M3's forward and backward pattern, in a popover and a bottom sheet alike. `afterSelect="back"` on a submenu's `Menu` makes a choice return to the page that opened it, which shows the new choice, instead of closing the menu. `portalContainer` opens the popover inside an element, kept within what shows of it, moving with it and shown with it in full screen, and placed again when it or the window resizes; an element that draws none of its children (a `<video>`) warns in development, and the page's `body` or `<html>` counts as none. The row above a page takes `classNames.header`, `back` and `title`, and the root menu's `backIcon`. `MenuSubmenuPresentation`, `MenuSubmenuTransition` and `MenuAfterSelect` are exported. Their code, with the bottom sheet's menu, loads when the browser is idle, on the trigger's hover or focus, or when the menu first opens, so a menu whose submenus open beside it doesn't carry it; a menu opened while its code loads closes on Escape, a press outside or focus moving on, a menu closed meanwhile never shows, and a failed load closes the menu and tries again on the next open (or, for a menu its app holds open, after a pause). Closing the whole menu from a page keeps the page, and focus in it, as the menu leaves. ([#102](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/102))
- `usePageHeight` in `@windmorph/react/primitives`, the Menu's measure of a page shown in a surface whose pages replace each other, taken in the same commit as the page changes, so a paged surface of an app's own springs its height from the first frame. ([#105](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/105))

### Changed

- Under reduced motion `useEntrance` is always entered (the preference is read as the element mounts, so a new setting reaches it on a remount, and an entrance entered under it stays entered), so an entrance no longer waits in its start state (an `AnimatedNumber` at its start, flat bars) until it is scrolled to, only to jump in: it shows its final picture at once. A fade-in that waits for `entered` doesn't play under reduced motion either. ([#100](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/100))
- `useLocale().locale` is the page's locale in a toolbar, list, tab or menu laid out in the other direction too (it was React Aria's locale in another script there, `en-Arab-US` on an `en-US` page), while `direction` stays the region's, so the two may not match there: read `direction` rather than working it out from the locale, and don't pass this locale to a provider of your own inside such a region. ([#101](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/101))
- A submenu shown in a menu's bottom sheet takes the root menu's `variant`, as a submenu shown in place in a popover does, so all of a menu's pages share one variant and a page never looks different from the one that opened it; it took its own. ([#102](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/102))

### Fixed

- The `dark:` variant follows the nearest part with a mode. Inside a light `ThemeScope` on a dark page (or a `system` page on a dark device) it no longer applies, and inside a dark scope within that light one it applies again, for scopes nested up to three deep. Its specificity is still a class's. ([#114](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/114))
- `Dialog`, `BottomSheet` and `SideSheet` take their first focus whatever opened them. Opened by a screen reader's click, or by Space on a native input such as a `Switch` or `Checkbox` (a click with no `detail`), they held no focus, which ended on the page. Their first control (or the `autoFocus` one, or a dialog's least destructive action) now takes it at the end of the enter motion, or at once under reduced motion. ([#113](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/113))
- `useEntrance` and `AnimatedNumber` show their final picture when the page is printed. A print runs no frames, so content never scrolled to, or mid-entrance, printed in its start state (flat bars, a line not drawn yet, a count at 0). On `beforeprint`, `useEntrance` enters and reports itself out of view until `afterprint`, committed in one render for every entrance on the page before the print is laid out (an entrance mounted during the print, as a print-only section an app renders from its own `beforeprint` handler, starts entered), so what React renders from it prints in its final state (start a print from a press or a `setTimeout`, not from inside an effect, which React can't flush); Motion writes styles bound to a Motion value on its next frame, which a print doesn't run, so a mark drawn from one needs a CSS print value (its resting geometry as a CSS variable, applied under `@media print`). `AnimatedNumber` prints its final value by CSS alone, so it holds even for a print started from an effect, which is laid out before React renders again. ([#100](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/100))
- The icon slots (IconButton, Button, FAB and FAB menu, chips, the split button, toolbars, tabs, navigation items, menus, switches, sliders, TextField, the search bar's leading icon, dialogs, list media and selection checks, and the pickers) now also size a single child that holds an `svg` directly, not only a bare `svg`: an icon in a wrapper of its own (an icon with a mark on its corner) shrank to nothing, and only the mark showed. Any other child keeps its own size: a `BadgedBox` keeps its 24px icon and its badge on the icon's corner, a letter or emoji stays centred, and in TextField's icons and the search bar's leading icon an app's own link or button keeps its size. A list item's leading and trailing slots, the search bar's trailing slot and `BadgedBox`'s anchor still size only a bare `svg`, since they hold media, avatars and controls of their own size. ([#103](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/103))
- `localeWithDirection` and `DomDirectionLocale` (used by the toolbars, lists, sliders, radio groups, button groups, menus, tabs, and the date and time fields) keep the locale's hour cycle and calendar. Only the script changed before, so `zh-Hant-TW` or `en-GB` regions in the other direction changed their clock; the tag now keeps whichever of these the new script would change (`zh-Hant-TW` → `zh-Arab-TW-u-hc-h12`), and React Aria still reads the requested direction from it. The digits are the new script's in the tag (`ar-EG` → `ar-Latn-EG`), so an app's own React Aria number or date-segment input in a left-to-right region of an Arabic page still takes Latin digits; the components' own numbers there (a calendar's days, the time picker's dial, a progress indicator's value) are written in the page's digits. ([#101](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/101))
- Inside a toolbar, list, tab or menu laid out in the other direction, the components write and read their dates, times and numbers in the page's locale. Such a region gives React Aria the locale in another script for its arrow keys, which formats as the language's default, so an `en-GB` `DateField` in a right-to-left region showed MM/DD/YYYY and saved 03/04/2030 as 4 March, and `fr-CA`'s 15 h 05 came out 15:05. The date and time fields, the pickers' headlines, weekdays and days (each day's name included), Slider's value, Badge, Carousel, `AnimatedNumber` and the typeahead of `Menu`, `Select` and `List` now keep the page's locale there; only React Aria's own words, such as "selected" around a day's date or the calendar grid's month name, follow the changed one. ([#101](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/101))
- A `Menu` inside a `MenuTrigger` has one name in the popover and the bottom sheet alike. With its own `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` (`aria-labelledby` when it has both) it's named by it in both; without, it's named after its trigger in both. The popover read the trigger's name ("More options") while the compact window's sheet read the menu's own ("Settings"). ([#99](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/99))
- An overlay opened in a container of the caller's (`Overlay`'s `portalContainer`) or of an app's React Aria `UNSAFE_PortalProvider` carries only the theme attributes that differ from what the container inherits, as in the element in full screen, so an app's own `:root` token values reach a menu or dialog there; it wrote every attribute, which declared the theme's values again. In a menu, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down only move focus: their default no longer scrolls an element around the menu. In a bottom sheet, a menu's first page fades in again as it shows after Back, as its other pages do. In a menu's bottom sheet, an action or a choice on a submenu's page keeps focus on the page as the sheet closes, instead of dropping it to the page's `<body>` until the sheet has gone; Escape and Left (Right in right to left) on the back button go back one page instead of closing the sheet; a submenu's own `className` and `style` reach its page; a sheet whose code fails to load closes, rather than leaving its trigger open over nothing, and tries again on the next open; and in a right-to-left region of a left-to-right app, the sheet's arrow keys follow the region (Right opened a page there, and Left did nothing). A `Select` whose bottom sheet fails to load closes the same way, and tries again on its next open, as do a `TimeField` and a `DateField` whose picker fails to load. A menu's bottom sheet, a `Select`'s, a `TimeField`'s dialog or docked list and a `DateField`'s compact dialog, opened while its code loads, close on Escape, a press outside or focus moving on, leave out `aria-controls` until there's something to point at, and no longer open and take focus from wherever it went once the code comes; an arrow key on the trigger keeps such a menu open, a window or tab switch no longer closes it, typing in a field's text box dismisses its full-screen picker, and a press in a context menu's region dismisses its menu, as do typing and ↓ / ↑ there (the arrow keeping its own use), or on the page when the region takes no focus; only a Tab from the trigger counts as moving on when focus then leaves for nothing. `ButtonGroup`'s overflow menu does the same while it loads, and closes when it fails to load. ([#102](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/102))
- `ThemeProvider` stores only the person's own selection. A dimension the app controls (a `mode` passed while the page is open) is no longer written to the cookie or localStorage; the value stored for it stays, so the person's choice comes back when the app stops passing it. `serializeThemeState` takes a partial state and leaves out the dimensions not given. ([#108](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/108))

## 0.2.10 — 2026-11-18

### Added

- Motion on values in `@windmorph/react/primitives`: `useM3SpringValue` and `useM3SpringValues` spring a number or a list of numbers on the theme's springs, keeping their speed when a new target arrives (`resample` reads a list at another length, and `keys` match a list by name, so a sliding window's kept elements carry on, with `rest` setting where a new one starts); `AnimatedNumber` counts between values; `staggerDelays` and `useEntrance` play an entrance one item after another, once, when it is first seen. ([#96](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/96))
- `useNearestReveal` in `@windmorph/react/primitives`, for collections built on React Aria's hooks outside the core: arrow keys bring an item into view without first centring the whole collection, so the page moves only as far as the item needs. ([#96](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/96))
- `themeSelector` in `@windmorph/react/primitives`, the selector of one theme, contrast level and mode in the theme CSS, for values of your own that follow the themes as the colour roles do. ([#96](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/96))

### Changed

- Needs `motion` 14.12 or later (the peer range is now `^14.12.0`): the motion primitives rely on its `useTransform` catching up with a value set as it subscribes again. ([#96](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/96))

### Fixed

- A polite announcement waiting for its live region (a focused button turning pending) writes through the region's own document, so it no longer throws when its page goes away first. ([#96](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/96))

## 0.2.9 — 2026-11-16

### Added

- A `Carousel`'s items can be links (`itemHref`, with `itemTarget` and `itemRel`): real links a `RouterProvider` routes, that a modifier or the context menu opens in a new tab, named by their content, with `classNames.link`. Each item now says its size, `data-size` (`large`, `medium` or `small`) and `--m3-carousel-item-openness` (0 to 1), so words over an item can fade as it shrinks to a strip, and `onFocalChange` names the item in the large place once scrolling settles (multi-browse and hero). ([#90](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/90))
- The library's consistent names for props that had two: `heroAlign` on `Carousel`, `verticalAlign` on `ListItem`, `itemAlign` on `NavigationRail` (with a `data-item-align` attribute), `current` and `currentIcon` on `NavigationBarItem` and `NavigationRailItem`, and `openDelay` on `TooltipTrigger`, `RichTooltipTrigger` and `SubmenuTrigger`. ([#88](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/88))
- `controlPlacement="end"` on `Switch`, `Checkbox`, `Radio` and `RadioGroup` puts the control after its label, which grows to fill the row: a settings row with its words at the start and its switch at the end, the whole row being the label. `start` (before the label) stays the default. The same name as `ListItem`'s; the `ControlPlacement` type is exported, and the `SelectionControl` primitive (`/primitives`) takes it too, for selection controls built outside the core. ([#90](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/90))
- `basePath` and `trailingSlash` on `NextRouterProvider`, the app's settings from `next.config`, so library links show the address Next's own `Link` writes (the base path in front, and the trailing slash `trailingSlash` asks for); navigation still goes through the router, which adds the base path itself. ([#92](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/92))
- `useSliderTouchTrack` in `@windmorph/react/primitives`, the Slider's touch handling for a slider of your own built on React Aria's `useSlider`: a tap sets the value as the finger lifts, a drag starts after 8px along the track, and a swipe across it scrolls the page. Beside it, `sliderThumbInputProps` makes React Aria's thumb input props right for assistive technology (named by a visible label, no empty descriptions, a screen reader's change committed) and `sliderArrowRaises` tells which arrow keys raise the value, mirrored right to left. ([#90](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/90))
- `valueText` on `Slider` and `RangeSlider` puts the value in words for screen readers (each thumb's `aria-valuetext`) where a number format can't, such as a time: "1 minute 25 seconds of 10 minutes". It gets each thumb's index too, so a range can say which end it is. The value indicator keeps `formatOptions`. ([#90](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/90))
- `ThemeScope` takes `as="span"`, so a scope can sit in content that takes only phrasing content (a `<button>`, a `<p>`, a `<label>`) without a `div` there. Its `ref` is typed for either element. Everything else works the same, overlays opened inside included. ([#90](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/90))

### Changed

- Needs `react-aria` 3.53.1 and `react-stately` 3.51.0 or later (were 3.52.1 and 3.50.0): the modifier-key focus rules follow React Aria 3.53's, which show an invalid field's ring after a failed form check. ([#94](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/94))
- `Snackbar`'s `onClose` is its close callback, the name `onDismiss` had (which stays as a deprecated alias): given, the snackbar shows its close (×) button, and the button and Escape call it. It used to reach the root element as a DOM attribute and did nothing, so a snackbar that passed `onClose` now shows the close button. ([#93](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/93))
- The recipes (`buttonStyles` and every other `*Styles` export) run on tailwind-variants 4, with the same merger as `cn`, so they produce the same classes as before. An app that imports `tailwind-variants` itself can move to 4 too. ([#92](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/92))

### Deprecated

- `classNames.trailing` on `AssistChip`, `SuggestionChip` and `InputChip`. It landed on nothing, since only a filter chip has a trailing icon; `FilterChip` keeps it for its `trailingIcon`. The docs' props tables leave deprecated keys out. ([#86](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/86))
- The old names of those props, which still work and go in a later minor release: `heroAlignment`, `verticalAlignment`, `itemAlignment` (and the `data-item-alignment` attribute), the navigation items' `selected` and `selectedIcon` (they mark the current page with `aria-current`, not a selection), `Snackbar`'s `onDismiss`, and the triggers' `delay`. Editors strike them through, naming the replacement; when both are given, the new name wins. ([#88](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/88))

### Fixed

- In a multiple `Autocomplete`, rows of input chips are 48px apart, top to top (16px between them), so a chip no longer takes presses meant for the chip above it. Each further row adds 48px to the field instead of 40px. ([#86](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/86))
- In a `Carousel`, Enter or Space on an item right after a mouse drag across it activates the item (only the click that ends a drag is ignored), and a mouse drag that starts on a picture scrolls the carousel instead of dragging the picture away. ([#92](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/92))
- After a press on a `Carousel` item and then a press elsewhere, Tab back into the carousel brings the focused item into view again; the press's "don't move" flag lasted until a key was pressed inside the carousel. ([#93](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/93))
- A focused chip at either end of a scrolling `ChipSet` (`layout="scroll"`) shows its whole focus ring. The row's 4px padding at each end cut 1px off the 5px ring; it now pads by the ring's own size, and a chip that focus scrolls into the row keeps its ring in view. ([#86](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/86))
- Links inside a `RouterProvider` with a `useHref` (an app's base path, say) show the router's address, as React Aria's own links do, so opening one in a new tab, copying it or a crawler reaches the right page. `Button`, `IconButton`, `Fab`, `ExtendedFab`, `ToolbarFab`, `Card`, `AssistChip`, `NavigationBarItem`, `NavigationRailItem`, `SplitButton`, `FabMenuItem`, link `ListItem`s and `ButtonBase` showed the raw `href`, though a plain click already went to the right page. A disabled link still has no address.
- A modifier key pressed alone no longer shows the focus ring of a control a pointer focused. On a Mac, Option alone (a shortcut's start, a window switcher) counted as keyboard use and left a ring behind; Caps Lock did everywhere. A key that does something, Escape and Tab included, still shows it, and a screen reader's focus keeps its rings. ([#89](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/89))
- Closing a dialog, sheet, menu or other overlay gives focus back to where it was without passing through the page. Focus was on `<body>` for one frame first, which a screen reader can announce as the page. It holds when a menu's action closes the dialog it was opened from too, and an app listening for React Aria's `react-aria-focus-scope-restore` event can still take it over. ([#86](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/86))
- An icon button or FAB in a dialog, menu or sheet opened from a button that has an app's tooltip shows its own label tooltip again. The tooltip trigger reached into the overlay, so a full-screen dialog's Close button and a sheet's close and back buttons showed no tooltip. An overlay now leaves behind every trigger it was opened from, so the action of a rich tooltip whose trigger sits inside a menu trigger no longer takes the menu trigger's props. ([#86](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/86))
- Tooltips, menus, sheets and dialogs show while an element is in full screen. They were portalled to `body`, which a browser in full screen doesn't draw; an overlay opened in full screen now goes into the element in full screen (unless it is a video or another element that draws no children, or the whole page) and stays there until it closes, so nothing open is rebuilt as full screen starts or ends, and an element inside an open dialog can go full screen. Its theme follows the element it is drawn in, so an app's own colours still reach it. A container given to the overlay, or one an app sets for a subtree with React Aria's `UNSAFE_PortalProvider`, still comes first. ([#89](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/89))
- Each option of a `RadioGroup` takes presses over its whole 48px touch target. Rows were 40px apart (34px at density -3), so a press on the bottom 4px of one option's control chose the option below. They are now 48px apart, top to top, at every density (8px between rows at the default density, 20px at -3), as a wrapping `ChipSet`'s rows are, so a radio group is 8px taller per option at the default density (14px at -3). ([#86](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/86))
- Escape in an `Autocomplete` with its list open, inside a bottom sheet, closes only the list. It closed a modal sheet along with it, and lowered an expanded standard sheet. A modal bottom sheet now closes on Escape as `Dialog` and `SideSheet` do, only when it is the topmost overlay; a standard sheet leaves an Escape to any control inside it whose popup is open. ([#86](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/86))
- A `Slider` or `RangeSlider` moved by a screen reader (VoiceOver's swipe up or down) calls `onChangeEnd`, as a key does. Before, only `onChange` came, so an app that commits on `onChangeEnd` never saw the change. ([#92](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/92))
- A touch on a `Slider` reads the slider as it is now rather than as it was when the finger went down, so a range that changes during a drag maps the finger to the new one, and a tap still waiting to tell itself from a drag is dropped once the slider is disabled, so it never sets a value the slider no longer takes. ([#94](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/94))

## 0.2.8 — 2026-11-15

### Fixed

- Escape pressed in a dialog or sheet opened from inside a `BottomSheet` no longer closes the bottom sheet behind it (or collapses an expanded standard one). The sheet handles Escape itself, and took the key from an overlay inside it that doesn't close on Escape (`closedBy="none"`, a dialog while it saves): the sheet closed, and the overlay with it. Escape that cancels a text being composed is left to the input method too. ([#84](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/84))
- A `Dialog` inside the content of a dialog that a `DialogTrigger` opened keeps its own open state. It took the trigger's instead of its own `open`, so it opened together with the outer dialog; each then made the other inert, and nothing in either could take focus or be pressed. A form's own dialog (an "Add a link", a "Discard changes?") now stays closed until its `open` says so, and closes alone. A modal `BottomSheet` or `SideSheet` inside a sheet that a `SheetTrigger` opened does the same, and so does a `Menu` or `Select` shown as a sheet there: after its first opening it followed the outer sheet, stayed up after a choice, and closing it closed the outer sheet. The outer panel alone now carries the `id` its trigger's `aria-controls` names. ([#82](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/82))

## 0.2.7 — 2026-11-14

### Fixed

- In a scrolling `ChipSet`, the arrow keys bring the whole of a chip with a remove button into view, its focus indicator included. Only the button that took focus was revealed, so the chip's other end could stay past the row's edge. ([#77](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/77))
- A `ChipSet` with `disabled` disables every chip in it. It reached only the filter chips that take part in a selection, so with the default `selectionMode="none"` the group announced itself disabled (`aria-disabled`) while every chip still worked. In a set with a selection, the assist, suggestion and input chips, and filter chips without a `value`, stayed enabled; they are disabled now too, and a chip cannot opt out, so keep one that must stay usable, such as a chip that clears the filters, outside the set. A set inside a disabled set is disabled as well. A chip's own `disabled` still works in a set that isn't disabled. ([#74](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/74))
- `ChipSet` calls `onSelectionChange` only when the selection changes. A press on the last selected chip of a set with `deselectable={false}` reported the selection it already had. ([#76](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/76))
- Enter on a day in a date picker dialog saves that day. The day was picked only when the key was let go, after the dialog had already saved, so a `PickerDialog` with `onConfirm` around a `DatePicker` or a `DateRangePicker`, and a `DateField`'s full-screen dialog, closed with the date picked before. The Enter that saves now picks the day as the key goes down. ([#77](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/77))
- A `FilterChip` keeps its selection and keyboard focus when `onRemove` is added or taken away, as an app does to show the remove button only on wider windows. The chip is one button without `onRemove` and a container of two with it, so the toggle was built anew: an uncontrolled chip went back to unselected, and focus fell to the page. ([#76](https://github.com/XManhTrinh/windmorph/pull/76))
- Enter or Space held long enough to repeat acts once in three places. On a time picker's dial, picking an hour moves focus to the minutes, where the repeat picked a minute too and moved the time to the nearest five; in a search bar, the repeat of the Enter that opened the view submitted the query as it stood; on a bottom sheet's drag handle, it went on to the next size and closed a modal sheet it had just opened fully. ## 0.2.5 — 2026-11-12

### Added
