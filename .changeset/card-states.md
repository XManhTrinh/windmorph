Card follows the M3 Expressive spec's states: outlined cards rise to level 1 on hover and get an `on-surface` border when keyboard-focused, pressed and focused cards drop back to their resting elevation, a disabled outlined border is `outline` at 12%, and the new `dragged` prop raises a card being moved (level 3, elevated level 4).
