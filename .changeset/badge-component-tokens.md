Badge has component tokens: `--md-comp-badge-color` (the small dot), `--md-comp-badge-large-color` and `--md-comp-badge-large-label-text-color` restyle every badge from CSS, globally, per theme or within any ancestor. Defaults are unchanged, and `className` still wins. The root carries `data-tokens` next to its `data-size`, and `badgeTokenSet` is exported.
