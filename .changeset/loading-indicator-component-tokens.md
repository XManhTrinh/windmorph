LoadingIndicator has component tokens: `--md-comp-loading-indicator-active-indicator-color`, `--md-comp-loading-indicator-contained-active-indicator-color` and `--md-comp-loading-indicator-contained-container-color` restyle every loading indicator from CSS, globally, per theme or within any ancestor. Defaults are unchanged, and `className` still wins. The root carries `data-tokens` and a public `data-variant` hook, and `loadingIndicatorTokenSet` is exported.
