Component tokens for the navigation components, menus and lists, with M3 Expressive's current names: Tabs (`--md-comp-{primary,secondary}-navigation-tab-*`), NavigationBar and NavigationRail (`--md-comp-nav-bar-*`, `--md-comp-nav-rail-*`, including their items), TopAppBar and SearchAppBar (`--md-comp-app-bar-*`), DockedToolbar and FloatingToolbar (`--md-comp-toolbar-docked-*`, `--md-comp-toolbar-floating-*`), SearchBar (`--md-comp-search-bar-*`, `--md-comp-search-view-*`), Menu and the option lists of Select and Autocomplete (`--md-comp-menus-*`, `--md-comp-menus-standard-*`) and List (`--md-comp-list-list-item-*`). Defaults are unchanged, and `className` still wins. New public styling hooks: `data-variant` on Tabs, tabs, TopAppBar, menu items and List items and roots; `data-color` on FloatingToolbar; `data-layout` on navigation items; `data-modal` on the modal rail's sheet. The sets are exported as `tabsTokenSet`, `navBarTokenSet`, `navRailTokenSet`, `appBarTokenSet`, `dockedToolbarTokenSet`, `floatingToolbarTokenSet`, `searchBarTokenSet`, `searchViewTokenSet`, `menuTokenSet` and `listTokenSet`. Code that builds on `menuStyles` or `listStyles` directly must put `data-tokens` and `data-variant` on its surface and items, as the components do.
