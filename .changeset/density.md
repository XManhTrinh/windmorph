Web density (M3), levels `0` to `-3` (`Density`, `DENSITIES`). `ThemeProvider` takes `density` / `defaultDensity` / `onDensityChange` (stored with the theme; `useTheme()` has `density` and `setDensity`, `ThemeScript` and `getThemeFromCookies` read it), `ThemeScope` takes `density`, and both write `data-density`, which sets the new `--md-sys-density` token for their subtree; overlays take the density of where they open. `TextField` (text areas too), `Select`, `Autocomplete`, `DateField`, `DatePicker` / `DateRangePicker` (input mode) and `TimePicker` (input mode) take a `density` prop and otherwise follow the theme: each step takes 4px off the container (56px to 44px; the time input 72px to 60px) and 2px off the padding above and below, the labels move to match, and a field keeps a 48px hit area. A searchable `Select`'s search field takes 4px a step off too (48px to 36px). `Menu` and the select and autocomplete option lists take 4px a step off their items (44px to 32px) and 2px off the space around dividers; `Menu`'s `density` now inherits from the theme when left out. Calendars and the time dial keep their size. Density 0 renders exactly as before.
