Divider has a component token: `--md-comp-divider-color` restyles every divider (including `LabeledDivider` and the dividers in menus) from CSS, globally, per theme or within any ancestor. The default is unchanged, and `className` still wins. The root carries `data-tokens`, and `dividerTokenSet` is exported.
