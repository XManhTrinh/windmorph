`TopAppBar` has a `bottom` slot for an accessory row such as filter chips: it's part of the bar, takes its colour, stays below the collapsed top row and hides with the bar. New `scrolledColor` prop: `container` (the default, `surface-container`) or `transparent`, which lets content show through the bar on scroll (give its icon buttons a container fill). `TopAppBarScrolledColor` is exported.
