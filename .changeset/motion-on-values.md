Added: motion on values in `@windmorph/react/primitives`: `useM3SpringValue` and `useM3SpringValues` spring a number or a list of numbers on the theme's springs, keeping their speed when a new target arrives; `AnimatedNumber` counts between values; `staggerDelays` and `useEntrance` play an entrance one item after another, once, when it is first seen.
